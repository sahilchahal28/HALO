📲 How to install as Native App (No Browser):

  1. Tap on Doctor or Patient button above to open the portal.
  2. On Android (Chrome): Tap the "Install" banner at the top (or tap Chrome menu ⋮ → "Install App").
  3. On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (⬆️) at the bottom, scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
  4. Open the newly installed app from your home screen to use it in 100% full-screen native mode!
